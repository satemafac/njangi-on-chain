Cycle 1 · six members

Everyone pays in. One takes the whole pot.

Each contribution is the member's own. It lands in a shared pot the whole group can see.

Cycle 2 · same circle

Next cycle, the next member.

Same pot, same rules, a different turn. That is the entire system, and it is older than any bank.

Cycle 3 · the part that matters

Nobody holds the pot. Not even us.

There is no company in the middle and no admin button that moves the money. Not a promise. A design.
