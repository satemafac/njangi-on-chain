Who the bank leaves out

Farida Nabourema, in her own words

70%of Africans, no bank account · rural: 95%

more than 70 % of our people are not banked. And when you go to rural areas that represent the largest chunk of our population, about 60%, more than 95 % of rural populations don't have access to bank accounts.

Togo to Ghana. Neighbours.

Farida Nabourema, in her own words

So you want to send money from Togo to Ghana, those are two neighboring countries, the money has to leave your bank in Togo, go to an intermediary bank in Europe before coming back to Ghana, that process alone costs time and money.

What moving money costs

Farida Nabourema, in her own words

Europe~2%
Africaup to 16%

more than 17 billion dollars are lost in remittance fees in Africa every year. And in other regions like Europe, remittance fees stand at around 2%. In Africa, it can go as high as 16%.
