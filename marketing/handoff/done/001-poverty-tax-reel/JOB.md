"The poorer you are, the more expensive the bank is to you."

Farida Nabourema calls it the poverty tax. In her own words: most Africans have no bank account. Money sent from Togo to Ghana, two neighbours, goes through a bank in Europe first. Africa loses over $17 billion a year to remittance fees. And when people need a solution, they find their way to it on their own.

They already did. A circle of people who trust each other: everyone pays in, one takes the pot, it goes round. Njangi, tontine, susu.

We built that circle to run across borders, with one rule it never had before: nobody holds the pot. No bank in the middle, no cut of the pot, no function that lets us move a circle's money.
