## Series design system (locked for every episode)

Palette:
- Ink: #101210 (backgrounds)
- Gold: #D9A441 (accents, key words, the circle motif)
- Ivory: #F4EDDE (primary type)
This palette is locked for the whole series. If Stalanic remaps it to the
Njangi brand palette, the remap then stays locked.

Typography:
- Headlines: high-contrast serif, heritage feel. Generous tracking and
  line spacing. Never more than ~6 words on screen at once.
- Captions: clean grotesque, word-timed to the VO.
- Key object centered every scene. Nothing decorative that does not serve
  the idea. No effect experiments, no hype music.

Motion rules:
- Eased keyframes everywhere, overlapping. No linear motion.
- Smooth dynamic transitions between scenes; hard cuts only if the message
  needs one (scene 1 -> 2 earns one: scale vs. the bank).
- Adaptive rhythm: slow and cinematic on heritage beats (scenes 1, 2, 6),
  a touch quicker on the mechanic (scenes 3-5), confident lift on the
  on-chain turn (scene 8).
- Mechanic motion language (scenes 3-5) matches Ep. 1 exactly, so the
  series is recognizable from any frame.

Music and sound:
- 60-80 BPM, cinematic heritage lane. Same family every episode.
- VO-led. Minimal SFX, only transitional. If an effect feels off, cut it.

Signature closer (every episode): the on-chain turn, then the series title
card "Circles of the World", same layout as Ep. 1.

## Scene-by-scene build (SCRIPT.md)

1. "Ajo. Nigeria." (~6s)
   VO: "Ajo. The Yoruba word for thrift. Across Nigeria, millions of people
   run their savings on it."
   Visual: dark ink background, gold serif "ajo" large and centered, ivory
   "Nigeria" beneath. Grounded, assured.

2. "The bank never came." (~6s, hard cut from scene 1)
   VO: "The bank never came for the market trader. The ajo did."
   Visual: a cold institutional facade motif dissolving or receding, while a
   warm gold circle forms in the foreground. The turn should feel like a
   quiet rebuttal.

3. "Everyone pays in." (~4s, rhythm picks up)
   VO: "It works like this. Everyone pays in."
   Visual: gold dots flowing into a circle from multiple directions.
   Same as Ep. 1. One idea, one motion.

4. "One takes the pot." (~4s)
   VO: "One person takes the whole pot."
   Visual: the circle's contents gather to a single gold point.
   Satisfying, clean.

5. "It goes round." (~5s)
   VO: "Then it goes round, until every member has had a turn."
   Visual: the gold point travels around the circle, lighting each member
   node in turn. Loop-friendly motion.

6. "The alajo's notebook." (~10s, slow back down, cinematic)
   VO: "Every morning, the alajo walks his round with a notebook, collecting
   a fixed sum from each trader. At the end of the cycle, each trader gets
   their lump sum. His fee: one day's contribution."
   Visual: warm photographic or painterly plate of a market morning; a
   ledger or notebook motif in gold. The human heart of the film.

7. "The catch: someone holds the pot." (~7s, tension)
   VO: "One catch. Someone has to hold the pot. And when the circle
   stretches across oceans, trust has further to travel."
   Visual: the gold circle stretches, thins, a single node holding the pot
   highlighted. Slight unease in the motion, then resolve.

8. "Nobody holds the pot." (~8s, confident lift)
   VO: "We kept the circle and removed that one risk. Same ajo, one new
   rule: nobody holds the pot. It runs on rules nobody can quietly break."
   Visual: the circle restores itself, no central holder, nodes connected
   directly. Gold on ink, assured. This is the brand beat.

9. "Circles of the World. Next week: the Paluwagan." (~6s)
   VO: "Circles of the World. Next week: the paluwagan. Link in bio to
   start your circle."
   Visual: series title card. Same layout as Ep. 1.
