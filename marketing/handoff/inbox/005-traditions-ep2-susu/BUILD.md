## One script change (fact-check). Please confirm.

Scene 1 VO: "One word from Ghana: susu. It means little by little." is now
**"One word from West Africa: susu. In Ghana's Twi, it means little by little."**
The Caribbean susu is widely traced to the Yoruba esusu (Bascom 1952; our own
/learn/esusu page), so opening on Ghana and then saying "it crossed the
Atlantic" credited the crossing to the wrong people. The Twi meaning stays,
attributed to Twi (Ghana's national press, MyJoyOnline, gives "little by
little" or "to plan" in Twi). The caption carries the same fix.

Checked and kept as written: "hand" for the payout (Trinidadian usage: members
"receive their hand"), "banker lady", Trinidad and Brooklyn.

Pronunciation guide fixed: Trinidad is TRIN-ih-dad (stress on the first
syllable), not trin-ih-DAD.
