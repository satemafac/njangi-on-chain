# VO-SCRIPT — Gemini TTS pack for Traditions Ep. 2: The Susu

Generate 9 separate clips, one per scene. Separate files make word-timed
captions exact with no alignment drift. Keep the punctuation as written:
periods are full stops, commas are short pauses.

## Voice direction (use as the TTS prompt)

"Deep, warm documentary voice. Measured, unhurried pace, around 130 words
per minute. Confident but never hypey. Think heritage documentary trailer,
not a commercial. Minimal processing, natural room tone."

## Pronunciation guide

- Susu: SOO-soo
- Twi: chwee
- Trinidad: trin-ih-DAD
- Ajo: ah-JOH

## Segments (exact text — read verbatim)

### vo-01.mp3 — warm, intimate, slow
One word from Ghana: susu. It means little by little. A plan built from
small pieces.

### vo-02.mp3 — weight, history, slight lift on "never died"
It crossed the Atlantic centuries ago, and it never died. Trinidad.
Brooklyn. Anywhere the diaspora gathers.

### vo-03.mp3 — crisp, rhythmic, one punch
It works like this. Everyone pays in.

### vo-04.mp3 — crisp, rhythmic, one punch
One person takes the whole hand.

### vo-05.mp3 — crisp, rhythmic, one punch

### vo-06.mp3 — warm, human, unhurried (the heart of the film)
In Trinidad, a banker lady collects from her neighbors every week. When
your turn comes, the whole hand is yours at once. No bank. No paperwork.
Just trust.

### vo-07.mp3 — tension, slow down on the last line
One catch. Someone has to hold the hand. And when the circle stretches
across oceans, trust has further to travel.

### vo-08.mp3 — confident lift, peak conviction on "nobody holds the hand"
We kept the circle and removed that one risk. Same susu, one new rule:
nobody holds the hand. It runs on rules nobody can quietly break.

### vo-09.mp3 — clean sign-off
Circles of the World. Next week: the ajo. Link in bio to start your
circle.
