Ep. 2 of Circles of the World, our weekly series on how the world saves together.

Susu is a word from Ghana. It means little by little. A plan built from small pieces. The circle crossed the Atlantic centuries ago and never died: Trinidad, Brooklyn, anywhere the diaspora gathers.

Everyone pays in on a fixed schedule. One person takes the whole hand. It goes round until every member has had a turn. In Trinidad, a banker lady collects from her neighbors every week. No bank. No paperwork. Just trust.

The catch was always the same: someone had to hold the hand. We kept the circle and removed that one risk.
