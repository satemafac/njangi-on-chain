# Traditions Series — Episode 1: The Tontine — SCRIPT V2

Series: Circles of the World. Weekly: one savings tradition per episode,
ending with the on-chain angle. Format: 9:16 vertical Reel, ~60 seconds,
word-timed captions (Claude's render pipeline, Opus 5.5).
Status: v2, needs Stalanic's approval before anything is published.

## Script (voiceover + on-screen lines)

1. On-screen: 1653. Italy.
   VO: "In 1653, an Italian banker invented the tontine. Everyone paid in.
   The last one alive took everything."

2. On-screen: Africa rewrote it.
   VO: "Three centuries later, Africa took the same word and built the
   opposite: a circle where everyone gets their turn."

3. On-screen: Everyone pays in.
   VO: "It works like this. Everyone pays in."

4. On-screen: One takes the pot.
   VO: "One person takes the whole pot."

5. On-screen: It goes round.
   VO: "Then it goes round, until every member has had a turn."

6. On-screen: Douala, Cameroon.
   VO: "In Douala, a market woman puts in ten thousand francs every Monday.
   When her turn comes, she stocks her entire stall. No bank. No paperwork.
   Just trust."

7. On-screen: The catch: someone holds the pot.
   VO: "One catch. Someone has to hold the pot. And when the circle stretches
   across oceans, trust has further to travel."

8. On-screen: Nobody holds the pot.
   VO: "We kept the circle and removed that one risk. Same tontine, one new
   rule: nobody holds the pot. It runs on rules nobody can quietly break."

9. On-screen: Circles of the World. Next week: the Susu.
   VO: "Circles of the World. Next week: the susu. Link in bio to start
   your circle."

## Caption (draft, for publish step)

Ep. 1 of Circles of the World, our weekly series on how the world saves.

In 1653 the tontine was a gamble where the last one alive took everything.
Africa rewrote it into the opposite: a circle where everyone gets their
turn. Market women in Douala still run them to stock their stalls, no bank,
no paperwork, just trust.

The catch was always the same: someone had to hold the pot. We kept the
circle and removed that one risk.

Next week: the susu. Follow to catch every circle.
