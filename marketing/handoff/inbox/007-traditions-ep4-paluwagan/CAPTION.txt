Ep. 4 of Circles of the World, our weekly series on how the world saves together.

In the Philippines, the circle is called paluwagan. The contribution is the hulog. The payout is the sahod, the word for salary, because that is what it is: friends, family, and coworkers paying each other a salary, one at a time.

The order is drawn by lots or set by need, and everyone keeps paying even after their turn. The country's highest court has ruled it is not a game of chance. It is trust, organized.

The catch was always the same: someone had to hold the pot. We kept the circle and removed that one risk.
