# VO-SCRIPT — Gemini TTS pack for Traditions Ep. 4: The Paluwagan

Generate 9 separate clips, one per scene. Separate files make word-timed
captions exact with no alignment drift. Keep the punctuation as written:
periods are full stops, commas are short pauses.

## Voice direction (use as the TTS prompt)

"Deep, warm documentary voice. Measured, unhurried pace, around 130 words
per minute. Confident but never hypey. Think heritage documentary trailer,
not a commercial. Minimal processing, natural room tone."

## Pronunciation guide

- Paluwagan: pah-loo-WAH-gahn
- hulog: hoo-LOG
- sahod: sah-HOD
- Manila: mah-NIH-lah
- Tanda: TAHN-dah

## Segments (exact text — read verbatim)

### vo-01.mp3 — bright, inviting, lift on "sahod"
In the Philippines, the circle is called paluwagan. And the payout has a
name too. Sahod. The word for salary.

### vo-02.mp3 — warm realization, smile in the voice
Because that is what it is. Friends, family, and coworkers paying each
other a salary, one at a time.

### vo-03.mp3 — crisp, rhythmic, one punch
It works like this. Everyone pays in.

### vo-04.mp3 — crisp, rhythmic, one punch
One person takes the whole sahod.

### vo-05.mp3 — crisp, rhythmic, one punch
Then it goes round, until every member has had a turn.

### vo-06.mp3 — warm, human, unhurried (the heart of the film)
Every payday, the collector gathers the hulog. The order is drawn by lots,
or set by need. Everyone keeps paying, even after their turn. No bank. No
paperwork. Just trust.

### vo-07.mp3 — tension, slow down on the last line
One catch. Someone has to hold the pot. And when the circle stretches
across oceans, trust has further to travel.

### vo-08.mp3 — confident lift, peak conviction on "nobody holds the pot"
We kept the circle and removed that one risk. Same paluwagan, one new
rule: nobody holds the pot. It runs on rules nobody can quietly break.

### vo-09.mp3 — clean sign-off
Circles of the World. Next week: the tanda. Link in bio to start your
circle.
